How CurveForge works

What it does

  1. Launches a token on a Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve (DBC) - mints the token and initializes the curve in one transaction, paid for and owned by your connected wallet, not the platform's. The curve already is the liquidity: anyone can trade the moment the token exists, with no presale and no team allocation.
  2. Tracks the curve's progress toward its preset's migration threshold.
  3. Migrates to a real DAMM v2 pool automatically once the threshold is reached.
  4. Lets you withdraw accumulated trading fees - creator and partner fees, claimed separately.

Every action (launch, migrate, claim) requires an explicit click and confirmation - nothing runs automatically in the background.

Curve presets

Four categories, all quoted in either SOL or a real tokenized stock:

  • Fee-shape curves - Low fee, Default, Flat, Exponential, Long, and Compounding DAMM v2 - all SOL-quoted, migrating at 10 SOL accumulated in the curve. They differ in how the trading fee decays over time and, for one preset, how the migrated pool's fees are collected.
  • xStocks (Backed Finance) - quotes the curve directly against a real, cash-settled tokenized stock (AAPL, TSLA, NVDA, SPY) instead of SOL. 1 unit of quote already equals 1 real share - no price conversion involved.
  • Backpack Securities - a second, larger tokenized-stock issuer (a direct, 1:1 redeemable security entitlement, legally different from xStocks) - dozens of tickers, growing over time as new ones are verified on-chain and added.
  • Live-priced (Pyth) - picks a real stock (TSLA or QQQ) and anchors the curve's SOL-denominated market-cap targets to that stock's live Pyth price, instead of a fixed SOL number.

Real stock-quoted launches

Every stock-quoted preset is backed by a real, on-chain-verified mint: Token-2022, badged by Meteora for DBC use, with a consistent extension profile (permanent delegate, an inactive transfer hook, pausable config, scaled UI amount) confirmed individually for every single ticker before it's listed here - never assumed from a registry alone.

Public developer API

GET /api/dbc-pool/:address - read-only, CORS-open, works for any Meteora DBC pool on-chain (pool address or base mint), not just ones launched on CurveForge. Returns mint, migration status, live curve progress, migration threshold, and fee metrics.

Tested on mainnet

The full lifecycle - launch, curve completion, migration to DAMM v2, and fee claiming - has been run end-to-end on Solana mainnet with real transactions, not just simulated. Source is public on GitHub.
